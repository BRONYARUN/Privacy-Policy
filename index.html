Privacy Policy: Bronyarun

Last updated: 8 November 2026. The English text is the reference version; the Spanish and Russian versions are translations.

1. Who we are

Bronyarun (Automation, Integration, Optimization) is a small-business automation platform operated by BRONYARUN LLC, a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, USA (Florida Document Number L25000307943). Mailing address: 8504 Waukegan Rd, Apt 14, Morton Grove, IL 60053, USA. Privacy contact: privacy@bronyarun.com. Bronyarun is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Our role

Bronyarun serves businesses ("Customers") that connect their own Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts or Threads profiles. For the messages and comments a Customer receives, the Customer generally decides why the data is used and BRONYARUN LLC processes it on the Customer's behalf (as a processor). For Customer account data (connection data and access tokens) and for security and abuse prevention, BRONYARUN LLC acts as a controller. A data processing agreement is available to Customers on request at the address above.

3. What the service does

Bronyarun publishes posts for the Customer on the connected accounts, receives the comments and messages that people send to those accounts, sorts them (for example buyer, seller, general question, spam), replies automatically in a limited way and passes real inquiries to a person on the Customer's team. The service never contacts a person first: it replies only to people who wrote to the connected account or commented on its posts, once per comment, and in Direct messages only within the 24-hour window allowed by the platform. Where the service replies in a conversation, the conversation states that the assistant is an AI assistant, and a person can take over at any time.

4. Data we receive from Meta

  • From connected business accounts: Page, Instagram and Threads account IDs and names, usernames, and the access tokens granted through Facebook Login for Business, Instagram Login or Threads. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • From people who contact a connected account: the user ID assigned by Meta (page-scoped or Instagram-scoped), the username when Meta provides it, the text of direct messages and comments, timestamps, and a link to the conversation. We do not download attachments, but the raw event from Meta may contain attachment links. Contact details that a person voluntarily writes in the conversation (for example a name or phone number) are stored with the inquiry.
  • Analytics: reach, likes, comments, saves and similar counters of our own posts, used to see which posts work.

We do not receive passwords or payment card data, and we do not receive messages from accounts that have not contacted the connected business. Posts found by keyword search on Threads are not part of this service at present.

5. Why we use the data

(a) to deliver incoming messages and comments to the Customer's team as notifications; (b) to classify them and estimate their priority using automated tools, including third-party language models; (c) to reply to people who contacted the account; (d) to keep the connection to the Customer's accounts working (token renewal); (e) to protect against abuse and fix errors. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train models. Our agreements with AI providers are aimed at keeping data out of provider training, but we do not control those providers' internal practices; for that reason personal data is masked before it is sent to a model wherever the task allows it.

Classification and replies are automated and are not decisions that, on their own, produce legal or similarly significant effects for a person; a person at the Customer handles every real inquiry and can override the assistant.

6. Legal basis (EU/EEA/UK)

The legitimate interests of the Customer and of BRONYARUN LLC in answering inquiries sent to the Customer's accounts and in keeping the service secure, balanced against your rights; performance of our contract with the Customer. You may object to processing at any time (section 10). We keep our assessment of these interests on file and will provide a summary on request.

7. Who receives data

The Customer that received the message, and these providers acting on our behalf:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): hosting. Servers are in Nuremberg, Germany (European Union).
  • Google (Gemini API): language model for classification and replies.
  • DeepSeek: backup language model, used only with masked data.
  • Typesafe (Jev): decision service for message classification.
  • Telegram: notifications to the Customer's team in the Customer's own Telegram group.
  • Cloudflare: email routing for our privacy mailbox (only emails you send us).

We may also disclose data to authorities where the law requires it. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on an adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses, or on another mechanism permitted by law; details are available on request. A notification about an inquiry is copied into the Customer's own messaging group; we cannot delete messages inside the Customer's own tools.

8. How long we keep data

  • Raw events received from Meta: 30 days, then deleted.
  • Message and comment text, name and username in inquiry records: 30 days, then erased.
  • Records of conversations with the assistant, with the contact details a person gave: 180 days after the last activity, then deleted.
  • A record without text, name or username (the numeric Meta ID, class, score and dates) can remain to avoid repeated replies to the same person and for statistics. We delete it on request.
  • Access tokens: until the Customer disconnects the account or asks for deletion; then deleted.
  • Encrypted backups of the database: daily copies are kept for about 30 days on our server, and encrypted offsite copies are kept for up to 12 months. Data that you ask us to delete is deleted from the live system straight away and disappears from backups when they expire; backups are not used to restore deleted data into the live system.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures: tokens are encrypted at rest, webhooks are verified by signature, backups are encrypted, and access is limited to authorised staff. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and how to delete your data

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive a copy. Email privacy@bronyarun.com from the account you wrote to, or tell us your Meta user ID or username, so that we can find the data and confirm that it is yours. We answer within one month; where a request is complex we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you why. You can also use Meta's tool: remove the app in Facebook Settings, Apps and Websites. Meta then sends a deletion request to our callback (https://api.bronyarun.com/meta/data-deletion) and we return a confirmation code and a status page (https://api.bronyarun.com/meta/deletion-status?code=…). Deletion removes the stored events, inquiry records and tokens linked to your ID. You have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Customers can disconnect an account at any time; this deletes its tokens.

11. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly process their data. If you believe a child has contacted a connected account, write to us and we will delete the data.

12. EU/UK representative and California residents

BRONYARUN LLC is established in the United States. We do not currently have an establishment in the EU or the UK; if a representative under Article 27 GDPR is appointed, it will be named here. We do not sell or share personal information as these terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act; California residents can make access and deletion requests in the same way as described in section 10.

13. Changes and contact
