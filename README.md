1. What this app does
ri0O_aSis is an internal automation tool that helps the operator publish content to Instagram and Threads, read and reply to comments, and respond to direct messages on accounts owned or managed by the operator. The app uses the official Meta Instagram API and Threads API.
2. Data we collect
Through the Meta APIs, the app may access:
• Basic profile information of connected Instagram and Threads accounts (username, account ID, profile picture).
• Media published by the connected accounts and their metadata.
• Comments and replies on the connected accounts' posts, including the author's username and text.
• Direct messages sent to the connected Instagram accounts, including sender ID and message text.
• Access tokens required to call the APIs.
We do not collect payment data, passwords, or data from accounts that are not connected to the app.
3. How we use data
• To schedule and publish posts, images, and videos on behalf of connected accounts.
• To display, moderate, and reply to comments.
• To read and answer direct messages addressed to the connected accounts.
• To generate internal statistics on publishing and engagement.
We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except service providers listed below.
4. Storage and security
Data is stored on servers located in Germany (Hetzner Online GmbH, EU). Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shared. Access to data is limited to authorized personnel of the operator. We use HTTPS for all data transfers.
5. Service providers
• Meta Platforms (Instagram API, Threads API).
• Hosting: Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany).
• AI processing for drafting replies: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google (Gemini), DeepSeek, TypeSafe AI (Jev). Message text may be sent to this provider to generate a suggested reply; it is not used to train models.
6. Data retention
• Comments and messages: retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
• Published media metadata: retained while the account remains connected.
• Access tokens: deleted immediately when the account is disconnected.
